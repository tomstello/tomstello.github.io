Cover Story 2026 AAAS Newcomb Cleveland Prize

Durably reducing conspiracy beliefs through dialogues with AI

Costello, T.H., Pennycook, G., & Rand, D.

Science, 385, eadq1814 (2024)

In conversations with over 2,000 conspiracy believers, AI-driven dialogues reduced belief by ~20% on average—effects that persisted for at least two months. One in four participants completely reversed their stance.
