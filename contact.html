Prospective Students & Postdocs

I'm actively recruiting for the Viewpoints Lab. If you're interested in the intersection of AI and behavioral science, please send me an email with your CV and a brief description of your research interests. PhD applicants should apply through the CMU SDS program.

Media Inquiries

For interview requests or press inquiries related to my research on AI, misinformation, conspiracy beliefs, or political psychology, please reach out via email with details about your outlet and deadline.

Collaboration & Speaking

I'm always open to discussing potential collaborations, especially on projects at the intersection of AI and behavioral science. For speaking inquiries, please include details about the event, audience, and topic.
